Abstract

Human Touch is a sophisticated mobile application that serves as an assistive tool for individuals living with various disabilities to help them become more independent, secure, and have better control over their lives.

People with any types of disabilities (visual, auditory, physical, and cognitive) are faced with a wide range of issues associated with communications, emergencies, mobility, management of essential routines like taking medication and going to appointments.

However, as the need for assistive technologies grows with the increasing digitization of our world, a majority of the available applications target only one function and provide a limited service.

Human Touch solves this problem by integrating multiple services and functions into a single accessible platform. The application contains features like smart reminders, emergency aid working even offline, tools for effective communications, volunteer aid service, accessibility maps, and user-friendly interfaces designed especially for people with disabilities.

Human Touch was created with Flutter and Firebase frameworks. It provides responsive, secure, and accessible experiences for people with different disabilities via their mobile devices. With its assistance, individuals with disabilities will be able to perform everyday activities with ease while improving their communications, security, and social integration.

About Human Touch

Human Touch is a smart companion application created to improve daily life and independence for people with disabilities. The application provides reminders, AI communication, emergency support, accessible guidance, health support, and volunteer services in one easy-to-use platform.
